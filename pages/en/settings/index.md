## Currency

Choose the currency used to show amounts: Japanese Yen, US Dollar, Euro, British Pound, Canadian Dollar, Australian Dollar, Chinese Yuan, South Korean Won or New Taiwan Dollar.

> **Note**
> Recorded amounts are not converted. Only the currency symbol and decimal places change.

## Theme

Change the look of the app in **Settings** → **Theme**. Changes apply to the whole app right away.

![Theme](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/shibatype1999/myKakeibo-manual/main/images/en/theme.png)

### Skins

| Group | Skins |
| --- | --- |
| Colors and shapes | Standard, Cool, Cute, Warm, Icy |
| Textured backgrounds (Premium) | Tiles, Fluffy, Marble, Wood, Washi, Graph paper, Starry night, Denim, Brick, Polka dots |

- Textured skins are available with [Premium](premium). Tap a locked skin to preview it across the app.
- **Starry night** always uses a dark look, whatever the brightness setting.

### Theme color, brightness and background

- **Theme color** … The color of buttons and headings. Theme color and background tint can be chosen with the **Standard** skin.
- **Brightness** … **System**, **Light** or **Dark**.
- **Tint the background** … When off, the background is white (black in dark mode).

Check the look in **Preview**.

## Passcode lock

Require a 4- or 6-digit passcode when opening the app (free).

1. Open **Settings** → **Passcode lock**.
2. Turn on **Require a passcode to open the app**.
3. Enter a new passcode twice.

| Setting | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Passcode length | 4 or 6 digits |
| Lock after | How long after switching to another app the lock starts (Immediately, 1, 5 or 15 minutes) |
| Change passcode | Enter your current passcode, then set a new one. |

After 5 wrong attempts, you must wait 30 seconds before trying again.

> **Note**
> If you forget the passcode, the only way is to delete and reinstall the app (your data will be erased). We recommend keeping a backup.

> **Locking with Face ID**
> On iOS 18 or later, touch and hold the myKakeibo icon on the Home Screen and choose **Require Face ID** to lock the app with an iPhone feature. You can open it just by looking at it. The app's passcode lock is for people who don't use Face ID or have an iPhone older than iOS 18.

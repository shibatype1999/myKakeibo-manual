# Reminders
<!-- position: 10 -->
<!-- description: How to set reminders so you don't forget to record your spending. -->

Reminders send a notification at a set time so you don't forget to record your spending.

## Adding a reminder

1. Open **Settings** → **Reminders**.
2. Tap **+** at the bottom right.
3. Choose **Daily** or **End of month**, choose the **Notification time**, and tap **Add**.

The first time you add a reminder, you are asked to allow notifications. Choose **Allow**.

| Type | Notification |
| --- | --- |
| Daily | "Did you record your spending?" |
| End of month | "Is this month's budget up to date?" (on the last day of the month) |

## Turning on/off and deleting

- Use the switch in the list to turn a reminder on or off.
- Tap the trash icon to delete it.

> **Not getting notifications?**
> Check that notifications are allowed in the iPhone **Settings** → **Notifications** → **myKakeibo**.

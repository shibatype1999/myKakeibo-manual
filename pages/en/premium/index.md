# Premium
<!-- position: 14 -->
<!-- description: What myKakeibo Premium (monthly or yearly) includes, and how to subscribe, restore and cancel. -->

**myKakeibo Premium** is a monthly or yearly subscription. Recording, scanning, bulk input, reports and every other budgeting feature work without Premium.

## What Premium includes

| Feature | Description |
| --- | --- |
| No ads | The ad above the bottom tabs is no longer shown. |
| 10 textured skins | Tiles, Fluffy, Marble, Wood, Washi, Graph paper, Starry night, Denim, Brick and Polka dots. → [Settings](settings) |
| Automatic daily backup to iCloud | Saves to iCloud once a day when you open the app. → [Backup, restore and reset](backup) |

## Subscribing

1. Open **Settings** → **Premium**.
2. Choose **Yearly** or **Monthly**. The price is shown on the screen (the App Store price for your country or region).
3. Tap **Subscribe** (or **Start free for …** if a free trial is available) and complete the purchase on the App Store screen.

When it's done, "Premium is now active. Thank you!" appears, and Premium in Settings shows **Active**.

> **About automatic renewal**
> - Subscriptions renew automatically.
> - If there is a free trial, it renews at the price of the plan you chose when the trial ends. If you cancel during the trial, you are not charged.
> - Unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the next renewal date, it renews automatically.

## Cancelling

Cancel in the iPhone **Settings** → **Apple ID** → **Subscriptions**.
While Premium is active, you can also open it from **Settings** → **Premium** → **Manage subscription**.

After cancelling, Premium stays available until the next renewal date. Your records stay as they are.

## Restoring purchases

After changing iPhones or reinstalling the app, sign in with the same Apple ID and tap **Settings** → **Premium** → **Restore purchases**.

## Trying skins

Even without Premium, tap a locked skin in **Settings** → **Theme** to preview it across the app. It goes back when you close the Theme screen.

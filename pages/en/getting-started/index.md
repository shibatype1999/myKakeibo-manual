## Screen layout

Use the tabs at the bottom of the screen to switch between four screens. The app opens on **Record**.

![Bottom tabs](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/shibatype1999/myKakeibo-manual/main/images/en/tab-bar.png)

| Tab | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| Record | Enter income, expenses and transfers. The buttons at the top right let you scan receipts and use bulk input. |
| List | See each month's records on a calendar and in a list, and edit or delete them. |
| Summary | See your balance, income and expenses in monthly and yearly charts, along with your budget status. |
| Settings | Set up categories, accounts, budgets, backups, themes, Premium and more. |

> With the free version, an ad is shown above the bottom tabs. Ads are removed with [Premium](premium).

## Terms

| Term | Meaning | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Income / Expense | Money coming in or going out. | Salary, groceries |
| Transfer | Moving money between your own accounts. Not counted as income or expenses. | ATM withdrawal, e-money top-up, NISA contribution |
| Category | A broad group for income and expenses. | Food, Utilities, Salary |
| Subcategory | A breakdown of a category. | Food › Dining Out, Utilities › Electricity |
| Account | Where your money is. Choose it as **Paid from** or **Deposit to** for each record. | Wallet, Bank Account, Credit Card |
| Bulk input page | A page that groups items you enter every month, so you can enter them together or automatically. | Housing & Utilities, Phone & Subscriptions |

## What's ready from the start

When you first open the app, the following are already set up. You can rename or delete anything you don't use.

- **Categories** … 20 expense categories such as Food, Daily Goods, Housing, Utilities and Phone & Internet, and income categories Salary, Side Job, Extra Income, Investment and Other Income, each with subcategories.
- **Accounts** … Wallet, Bank Account, Secret Savings, Time Deposit, Employee Savings, Credit Card, NISA (Tsumitate), iDeCo, NISA (Growth), Mutual Funds and Stocks.
- **Bulk input pages** … Housing & Utilities, Phone & Subscriptions, and Loans & Insurance.

## Getting started

1. **Set up your accounts**
   In **Settings** → **Accounts**, add the accounts you use and enter the balance you start with.
   → [Accounts and transfers](accounts)
2. **Record your daily spending**
   On the **Record** tab, enter an amount, choose a category and tap **Save**. Scan receipts with the button at the top right.
   → [Recording income, expenses and transfers](recording) / [Scanning receipts and statements](scan)
3. **Set up fixed monthly costs (optional)**
   Group rent, utilities and savings on a bulk input page to enter them in a few taps each month, or automatically.
   → [Bulk input pages and automatic entries](bulk-input)
4. **Set a budget (optional)**
   Enter a monthly budget in **Settings** → **Budgets** to see how much is left in Summary.
   → [Budgets](budget)
5. **Look back with List and Summary**
   → [Checking and editing in the list](list) / [Summary and charts](summary)

> **Tip**
> All records are stored on your device. Back up from time to time in case you change or lose your iPhone.
> → [Backup, restore and reset](backup)

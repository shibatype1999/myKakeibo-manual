# Recording Income, Expenses and Transfers
<!-- position: 2 -->
<!-- description: How to enter income, expenses and transfers one at a time on the Record tab. -->

On the **Record** tab, switch between **Income**, **Expense** and **Transfer** at the top. **Expense** is selected when you open it.

![Record screen](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/shibatype1999/myKakeibo-manual/main/images/en/record.png)

Each tab has a **Single** / **Bulk** switch.

- **Single** … Enter one record at a time (explained on this page).
- **Bulk** … Choose a bulk input page and enter its items together. → [Bulk input pages and automatic entries](bulk-input)

Your choice is remembered for each tab. Switching does not clear what you are typing.

The two buttons at the top right:

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Scan (document icon) | Scan receipts and statements. → [Scanning receipts and statements](scan) |
| Bulk input (checklist icon) | Open the list of bulk input pages. |

## Recording an expense or income

1. On the **Expense** (or **Income**) tab, choose **Single**.
2. Enter the **amount**.
3. Tap the **Category** field and choose a category and subcategory (see "Choosing a category" below).
4. Choose the account in **Paid from** (**Deposit to** for income).
5. Enter the **date & time** and a **memo** if needed. For expenses, you can also enter the **store**.
6. Tap **Save**.

After saving, "Saved" appears and the amount, subcategory, memo and store are cleared. The category, account and date stay as they are so you can keep entering.

> **Tips**
> - The account you chose last time for that kind of record is selected automatically. At first, expenses use **Wallet** and income uses **Bank Account**.
> - Tap outside an input field to close the keyboard.

### Choosing a category

Tapping the category field opens a sheet of category icons.

1. Tap a category icon. Its subcategories appear below as "*Category* breakdown".
2. Choose a subcategory, or choose "*Category* only" to record without a subcategory.

![Choosing a category](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/shibatype1999/myKakeibo-manual/main/images/en/category-picker.png)

You can add and reorder categories and subcategories in Settings. → [Categories and subcategories](categories)

### Choosing an account

Tapping an account field opens a sheet of account icons, grouped into **Money** and **Investment**. Tap the account to use.
For income and expenses, only money accounts (cash, bank, card, e-money) are shown. Record money moved into investment accounts as a **Transfer**.

## Recording a transfer

A transfer is **money moved between your own accounts**, such as an ATM withdrawal, an e-money top-up, a NISA or iDeCo contribution, or a credit card payment. Transfers are not counted in income or expense totals.

1. On the **Transfer** tab, choose **Single**.
2. Enter the **amount**.
3. Choose the **Withdraw from** and **Deposit to** accounts.
4. Enter the **date & time** and a **memo** if needed, and tap **Save**.

**Frequent transfers** shows account pairs you used recently, plus pairs from your bank account to investment accounts, wallets and cards. Tap one to fill in both accounts.

> **Note**
> Choose two different accounts. A transfer to the same account cannot be saved.

## Fixing a record

Tap a record on the **List** tab to edit or delete it. → [Checking and editing in the list](list)

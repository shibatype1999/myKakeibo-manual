# Budgets
<!-- position: 7 -->
<!-- description: How to set a monthly spending budget, overall and by category, and check it in Summary. -->

You can set a monthly spending budget, both overall and by category. The same budget is used every month.

## Setting a budget

1. Open **Settings** → **Budgets**.
2. Enter an amount in **Monthly spending budget (overall)** and tap **Save**. Saving it blank removes the overall budget.
3. To set budgets by category, tap a category under **By category**, enter a monthly amount and tap **Save**. Leave it blank for no budget.

You can also open it from **Set a monthly budget** in the Balance month view on the **Summary** tab.

## Checking your budget

Go to **Summary** → **Balance** → **Month**.

![Budget status](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/shibatype1999/myKakeibo-manual/main/images/en/budget.png)

- **Budget for this month** … Shows a bar of what you have spent and "*amount* left" or "*amount* over". For the current month, it also shows the days left and how much you can spend per day. Tap the pencil at the top right to change the budget.
- **Budgets by category** … Shows a bar of what you have spent in each category.
- The Year view shows the number of months you stayed within your overall budget.

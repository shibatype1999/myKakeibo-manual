# Accounts and Transfers
<!-- position: 8 -->
<!-- description: How to manage cash, bank accounts, credit cards, e-money and investment accounts, how balances are calculated, and how transfers work. -->

An **account** is where your money is. When you choose an account as **Paid from** or **Deposit to** for each record, you can see the balance of each account.

## Account list and balances

Open **Settings** → **Accounts** to see each account's balance, with **Total assets** at the top. Accounts are grouped into **Money** and **Investment**.

![Account list](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/shibatype1999/myKakeibo-manual/main/images/en/accounts.png)

Balances are calculated as follows (confirmed records only):

> Starting balance + income − expenses − money transferred out + money transferred in

| Type | What the balance means |
| --- | --- |
| Cash, Bank, E-money | The amount you have now. |
| Card | Spending is shown as a negative amount. Recording the card payment as a transfer from **Bank Account** to **Credit Card** brings it back to 0. |
| Investment | The **invested principal** (gains and losses are not reflected). |

## Adding and editing accounts

1. On the **Accounts** screen, tap **+** at the bottom right (tap an account to edit it).
2. Set the following and tap **Save**.

| Setting | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Account name | e.g. ABC Bank, My Visa |
| Type | Cash, Bank, Card, E-money or Investment |
| Starting balance | The balance when you start using the app. For cards, enter unpaid charges as a negative amount. For investments, enter the principal you have already invested. |
| Icon, Color | The icon and color shown in lists and pickers. |

### Deleting an account

Tap the trash icon at the top right of the account editing screen. Records are kept, but will no longer be linked to that account.

## What is a transfer?

A **transfer** moves money between your own accounts. It is not counted as income or an expense; only the account balances change.

| Example | Withdraw from → Deposit to |
| --- | --- |
| ATM withdrawal | Bank Account → Wallet |
| E-money top-up | Bank Account (or Wallet) → e-money account |
| Credit card payment | Bank Account → Credit Card |
| NISA or iDeCo contribution | Bank Account → NISA (Tsumitate), etc. |
| Investing with a credit card | Credit Card → Mutual Funds, etc. |

Enter transfers on the **Transfer** tab of the **Record** screen. → [Recording income, expenses and transfers](recording)
Transfers to investment accounts appear as **Amount invested** in Summary. → [Summary and charts](summary)

> **Tip**
> When you shop with a credit card, record the expense with **Credit Card** as **Paid from**. On the payment date, record a transfer from **Bank Account** to **Credit Card**. This keeps both balances correct without counting the expense twice.

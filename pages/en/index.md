# myKakeibo User Guide
<!-- position: 2 -->
<!-- description: A guide to using myKakeibo, the household budget app. -->

myKakeibo is a household budget app that makes everyday entries quick and fixed monthly costs even easier.

![Record screen](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/shibatype1999/myKakeibo-manual/main/images/en/record.png)

## What you can do

- **Scan and record** … Read receipts, bank statements, and credit card or payment app histories from the camera or your photos. You can also import CSV or Excel files.
- **Enter fixed monthly costs at once** … Group rent, utilities, savings and more on a **bulk input page** and enter them in a few taps. They can also be recorded automatically on a set day each month.
- **Calendar list** … See daily totals on a calendar and tap any record to edit or delete it.
- **Reports and charts** … Category pie charts with percentages, monthly and yearly trends, a cumulative chart, and comparisons with the previous year.
- **Budgets** … Set a monthly budget (overall and by category) and see how much is left and how many months you stayed within it.
- **Accounts and transfers** … Manage cash, bank accounts, credit cards, e-money and investment accounts. Transfers between your own accounts are kept separate from income and expenses.
- **Make it yours** … Choose from 15 skins, theme colors, and light or dark mode.
- **Keep your data safe** … Back up on your device, lock the app with a passcode, and with Premium, back up to iCloud automatically.

All your budget data is stored on your device, and scanning is done on the device. No account is needed.

# Backup, Restore and Reset
<!-- position: 11 -->
<!-- description: How to back up to your device and iCloud, restore from a backup, and reset your data. -->

Your budget data is stored only on your iPhone. We recommend keeping a backup in case you change phones, your phone breaks, or something is deleted by mistake.

Open **Settings** → **Backup & Restore**.

![Backup & Restore](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/shibatype1999/myKakeibo-manual/main/images/en/backup.png)

## Backing up on this device (free)

1. Tap **Back up now**.
2. The backup is added to **Saved backups** with the date and time.

Backup files are saved in the Files app under **On My iPhone** → **myKakeibo** → **backups**.

> **Note**
> Backups on the device are lost together with the app if you lose your iPhone or delete the app. For important data, use the Premium iCloud backup, or copy the backup file to iCloud Drive or another location with the Files app.

### Restoring from a backup on this device

1. Under **Saved backups**, tap the restore button (circular arrow icon) next to the backup you want.
2. When asked "Restore?", tap **Restore**.

Your current data is backed up automatically before restoring (it appears in the list as **Auto-saved before restore**). If you restore the wrong one, you can restore from that backup.

Delete backups you no longer need with the trash icon.

## Backing up to iCloud automatically (Premium)

With [Premium](premium), you can back up to iCloud automatically every day.

- **Automatic backup** … When on, your data is saved to iCloud once a day when you open the app. The last 7 days plus 2 monthly copies are kept.
- **Last backup** … Shows when the last backup was saved.
- **Back up to iCloud now** … Saves to iCloud right away.
- **Restore from iCloud** … Choose a saved date to restore. Your current data is kept on the device automatically before restoring.

This is not available if you are not signed in to iCloud or iCloud Drive is off. Check the iPhone **Settings** → **Apple ID** → **iCloud**.

### When you change iPhones

1. On your old iPhone, tap **Back up to iCloud now**.
2. Install myKakeibo on your new iPhone and, signed in with the same Apple ID, tap **Settings** → **Premium** → **Restore purchases**.
3. In **Settings** → **Backup & Restore** → **Restore from iCloud**, choose the latest backup to restore.

Without Premium, use the Files app on your old iPhone to copy a backup file to iCloud Drive or elsewhere, put it in **On My iPhone** → **myKakeibo** → **backups** on the new iPhone, and restore it in the app.

## Resetting data

Use **Settings** → **Reset data** to delete data. Deleted data cannot be restored, so make a backup first if you need one.

| Option | What is deleted | What is kept |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Delete records only | All income, expense and transfer records | Categories, accounts, bulk input pages, budgets and settings |
| Delete everything | Records, categories, accounts, bulk input pages, budgets, reminders and settings such as the theme (the app returns to its first-launch state) | Nothing |

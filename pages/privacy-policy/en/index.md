# Privacy Policy

[日本語版](../privacy-policy)

The operator (the "Operator") of myKakeibo, the household budget app (Japanese name: マイ家計簿; the "App"), sets out this privacy policy (the "Policy") describing how user information is handled.

This Policy is a translation of the Japanese version. If there is any discrepancy between the two, the Japanese version prevails.

## 1. Basic policy

The App is designed so that the budget data you enter is kept only on your device and in your own iCloud. The Operator does not collect your income, expenses, accounts, balances or other budget data.

Because the App shows ads, information needed to deliver ads may be sent to an ad network (Google LLC). See "7. Advertising".

## 2. Data entered in the App

- Records (income, expenses and transfers), accounts, categories, tags, budgets, bulk input pages, memos, store names, settings and other data you register in the App ("Budget Data") are stored on your device.
- Budget Data is not sent outside your device except as described in "4. Use of iCloud". It is never sent to the Operator's servers.
- No account is needed to use the App, and the Operator never asks you for personal information such as your name or email address.
- Deleting the App deletes the Budget Data stored on your device, including backups on the device.

## 3. Use of device features

The App uses the following features only when you operate them or give permission.

| Feature | Purpose and handling |
| --- | --- |
| Camera | Used to photograph and read receipts and statements. Photos are deleted after reading and are not saved. |
| Photos and Files | Used to read images, PDFs and spreadsheet files (CSV, Excel) you choose, and for backups and CSV imports. |
| Share menu | Used to read images and PDFs sent to the App from other apps through the share menu. Files are deleted after reading. |
| Notifications | Used to show reminders and notices such as upcoming card payments. Notifications are scheduled on the device. |
| Shortcuts | If you set up automatic Apple Pay recording, the App receives the payment amount, merchant name and card name passed from the Shortcuts app and saves them as records awaiting confirmation. This information is processed on the device. |

### Text recognition and AI

- Text in images is read with on-device features (Apple's Vision).
- On devices that support Apple Intelligence, on-device AI is used to organize scan results, suggest categories and write reviews. Processing is done on the device, and Budget Data and images are not sent to the Operator or anyone else.
- You can turn off AI in the App's **Settings**.

## 4. Use of iCloud

The App uses Apple Inc.'s iCloud for the following features. When you use them, Budget Data is stored in your iCloud. The Operator cannot see data stored in your iCloud. Data in iCloud is handled under Apple Inc.'s privacy policy.

| Feature | What is stored and who can see it |
| --- | --- |
| Automatic iCloud backup (Premium) | Backup files of your Budget Data are stored in your iCloud Drive. |
| Sync between your devices with iCloud | Budget Data is stored in your iCloud (CloudKit) and delivered to your devices signed in with the same Apple ID. |
| Sharing with family | The Budget Data of a family budget, and the "Your name" you set (shown as the person who entered a record), are shared with the people you invite and who join. They can see the shared Budget Data and, depending on their permission, add to or change it. |

- For syncing and sharing, notifications that tell other devices about changes may be sent through Apple's services.
- You send invitation links yourself, using the method you choose (Messages, email, etc.).
- To stop sharing, stop sharing or leave the budget from **Sharing with family** in the App. Data already delivered to participants' devices before you stop sharing depends on the state of each device.

## 5. Purchasing Premium

- Subscriptions are purchased and paid through Apple Inc.'s App Store.
- The Operator does not collect payment information such as your credit card details.
- Purchase status is checked on your device using the App Store's mechanisms. Purchase information is handled under Apple Inc.'s privacy policy.

## 6. Exported files

CSV files you export and files you send through the share menu are saved to the destination you choose (the Files app, iCloud Drive, email, other apps, etc.). They are handled under the terms of each service.

## 7. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob, an ad service provided by Google LLC, to show a banner ad at the bottom of the screen. Ads are not shown while you use Premium.

- To deliver ads, information such as your IP address, device type, OS version, app information, and ad views and taps may be sent to Google.
- Google uses this information to deliver ads, measure ad performance and prevent ad fraud.
- Budget Data and scanned images are not sent to Google or any other ad network.
- The App does not ask for iOS tracking permission. It therefore does not use the iOS advertising identifier (IDFA) to track you across other companies' apps and websites.
- For how Google handles information, see the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

### How to limit the use of information for ads

- iPhone: Turning off **Settings** → **Privacy & Security** → **Tracking** → **Allow Apps to Request to Track** prevents apps from using the advertising identifier.
- You can also turn off personalized ads in [Google My Ad Center](https://myadcenter.google.com/).

## 8. Analytics

The App does not use analytics tools or crash report collection tools.

## 9. Disclosure to third parties

Because the Operator does not collect Budget Data, it never discloses it to third parties. With **Sharing with family** described in "4. Use of iCloud", Budget Data is shared with the people you invite. The information described in "7. Advertising" may be sent to Google to deliver ads.

## 10. Information you send when contacting us

Information you send when contacting us, such as your name, email address, message, and device and app versions, is used only to respond to you and improve the App, and is not disclosed to third parties except as required by law.

## 11. Changes to this Policy

The Operator may change this Policy when necessary. When it does, it will set an effective date and announce the changed Policy and its effective date on this page before that date.

## 12. Contact

For questions about this Policy, please contact us through the support link on the App's App Store page.

# Categories and Subcategories
<!-- position: 9 -->
<!-- description: How to add, edit, reorder and delete categories and subcategories, and how fixed monthly scheduled records work. -->

Income and expenses are grouped by **category** (e.g. Food) and **subcategory** (e.g. Dining Out). Many categories are ready from the start, and you can add or change them freely.

## Managing categories

Open **Settings** → **Categories**. Switch between **Income** and **Expense** at the top.

- **Add** … Tap **+** at the bottom right, enter a category name, choose an icon and color, and tap **Save**.
- **Edit** … Tap a category.
- **Reorder / delete** … Tap the pencil at the top right to enter edit mode, then drag ≡ to reorder and tap ⊖ to delete.

Deleting a category also deletes its subcategories. Related records are kept and shown as "(Deleted category)" in the list.

## Managing subcategories

Open **Settings** → **Subcategories**. Use **Income** / **Expense** at the top and the category chips below them to choose which category's subcategories to show.

- **Add** … Tap **+** at the bottom right, enter a subcategory name and tap **Save**.
- **Edit** … Tap a subcategory.
- **Reorder / delete** … Tap the pencil at the top right to enter edit mode, then drag ≡ to reorder and tap ⊖ to delete. Related records are kept.

## Fixed monthly (scheduled records)

When you add or edit a subcategory, turn on **Fixed monthly** and enter a **Fixed amount** to have a **Scheduled** record for that subcategory created automatically at the start of each month.

- Scheduled records are shown in yellow as **Scheduled** in the list. **They are not included in totals.**
- Tap a scheduled record in the list, check the amount and tap **Confirm** to turn it into a normal record.
- Saving the same subcategory from a bulk input page also confirms the scheduled record instead of creating a new one ("Saved N items (N scheduled items confirmed)").
- A scheduled record uses the same account as the last confirmed record for that subcategory.
- Scheduled records are created when you open the app. They are not created if the subcategory already has a record that month.

> **Tip**
> For items with the same amount every month that you don't need to check, **Register automatically every month** on a bulk input page is handy. → [Bulk input pages and automatic entries](bulk-input)

# Checking and Editing in the List
<!-- position: 5 -->
<!-- description: How to check each month's records on the List tab with a calendar and list, and how to edit or delete them. -->

The **List** tab shows a month of records on a calendar and in a list grouped by day.

![List screen](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/shibatype1999/myKakeibo-manual/main/images/en/list.png)

## Changing the view

- **Income / Expense / Transfer** … Tap the tabs at the top. **Expense** is shown when you open it.
- **Month** … Tap the arrows on either side of the month, or swipe left or right, to move to the previous or next month.
- **Show or hide the calendar** … Tap the handle below the calendar (or the monthly total bar). Your choice is remembered.

The monthly total (Total income, Total expenses or Total transfers) is shown above the calendar.

## Calendar

Each day on the calendar shows that day's total.

- Tap a date to show **only that day's records**. "Records on *date*" appears above the list.
- Tap the same date again, or the × on "Records on *date*", to go back to the whole month.

## Reading the list

Records are shown newest first, grouped by day. Each date heading shows that day's total on the right (Sundays in red, Saturdays in blue).

Each row shows the time, category icon, "Category / Subcategory" and amount, with the memo, store and account below.

| What you see | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Yellow row, **Scheduled**, hourglass icon | A scheduled record created from a fixed monthly subcategory and not yet confirmed. It is not included in totals. |
| **Auto** | A record added by a bulk input page's automatic registration. |
| From → To | A transfer. |

## Editing or deleting a record

1. Tap a record in the list to open **Edit record**.
2. Change the amount, category, account, date & time, memo or store, and tap **Save**.
3. To delete it, tap **Delete** and confirm. Deleted records cannot be restored.

### Confirming a scheduled record

When you tap a **Scheduled** record, "This is a scheduled record. Confirm it to include it in totals" is shown. Enter the amount and tap **Confirm** to turn it into a normal record that counts in your totals.

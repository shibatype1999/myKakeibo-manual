# Summary and Charts
<!-- position: 6 -->
<!-- description: How to check your monthly and yearly balance, category breakdowns and trends on the Summary tab. -->

The **Summary** tab shows your balance, income and expenses in charts.

![Summary screen](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/shibatype1999/myKakeibo-manual/main/images/en/summary.png)

## Changing the view

- **Balance / Income / Expense** … Tap the tabs at the top.
- **Month / Year** … Switch between a monthly and a yearly view below the tabs.
- **Period** … Tap the arrows on either side of the month (year), or swipe left or right.

Only confirmed records are included. Scheduled records and transfers are not counted as income or expenses.

## Balance tab

### Month view

- Shows the **Balance (income − expenses)**, income, expenses and the change **vs last month**.
- If you have a monthly budget, **Budget for this month** shows how much you have spent, how much is left or how much you are over. If not, you can set one with **Set a monthly budget**. → [Budgets](budget)
- In months with transfers to investment accounts, the **Amount invested** is shown.
- If you have budgets by category, **Budgets by category** shows how much of each you have used.

### Year view

- Shows the **Yearly balance** and a bar chart of the **Monthly balance**.
- The **Cumulative** chart shows how your balance adds up from January. Tap the chart to see the amount for that month.
- If you have an overall budget, the number of **Months within budget** is shown (e.g. 8 / 10 months).
- The total **Amount invested** is shown.

### Real balance

If you invest in funds with a credit card, that amount is included in your card payment (an expense), so your balance looks lower than it really is.
If you record transfers from a credit card to an investment account, a **Real balance** that adds those amounts back to your balance is shown with a breakdown ("Card-funded investments added back").

Transfers from a bank account to an investment account are not added back because they are never counted as expenses.

## Income and Expense tabs

### Month view

- Shows the month's total and a category **pie chart** with percentages.
- Below it, each category's amount and percentage is shown as a horizontal bar. The lighter bar is **last month's** amount.

### Year view

- Shows each category's monthly trend and the **Yearly total**.
- Use the buttons at the top right of the chart to switch between **Stacked bars**, **Line chart** and **Pie chart** (stacked bars and line chart show the top 8 categories).
- Tap a category to compare January to December for that category as horizontal bars. The lighter bar is the same month **last year**. The yearly total, monthly average and last year's total are also shown.
